# Software Work Moves Toward Domain-Verified AI Output

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
As AI systems generate more of the implementation layer, the limiting factor shifts toward verifying whether outputs satisfy real domain rules. The productive unit of work becomes a draft-plus-check loop, not just code generation. This tends to increase the value of domain experts, reviewers, and test writers who can judge correctness against operational truth.

## Supporting Data Points

- The source contrasts domain experts who can verify outputs with generalist engineers who may not detect plausible but wrong results.
- The source states that the agent does the transcription while humans do the judging.
- The source argues that code-generation skill has become less scarce than verified domain knowledge.

## Time sensitivity

Actionable as of 2026-05-30; relevant while agentic coding tools continue lowering the cost of generation faster than they lower the cost of correctness review.

## Uncertainty / maturity

This is a source-level argument, not measured evidence. It may not hold equally across every software domain because some systems still require substantial architecture and debugging beyond transcription.
