# Sales Moves from Fast Follow-Up to Agent-Led Intake

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
Sales orgs are shifting from optimizing how quickly humans respond to inbound interest toward using agents to handle first contact, qualification, and initial routing automatically. The operational center of gravity moves from response-time SLAs and SDR coverage to continuous intake, context capture, pipeline creation, and buyer experience during the first conversation. This reframes the frontline from a speed problem into an automation and handoff design problem.

- The article says every lead can be engaged immediately, at any hour and in any language.
