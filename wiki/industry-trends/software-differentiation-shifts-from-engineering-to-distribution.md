# Software Differentiation Shifts from Engineering to Distribution

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
As AI makes software building faster and more commoditized, companies may shift resources from implementation toward distribution functions such as marketing, sales, brand-building, and attention capture. The broader pattern is a change in what becomes scarce inside technology companies: not the ability to ship code, but the ability to get products noticed, adopted, and grown.

## Supporting Data Points

- Snap CEO Evan Spiegel explicitly predicts more resources will move from software engineering to distribution.
- The article says two-thirds of new code at Snap is now written by AI.
- The article argues that as software becomes cheaper and more commoditized, attention becomes the new scarce resource.
- The article cites Snap, Amazon, and JPMorgan as signs that AI is already changing how enterprises organize engineering work.

## Time sensitivity

Time-sensitive as of 2026-05-03; the claim depends on continued AI-driven productivity gains in software development and on whether distribution remains the binding constraint for growth.

## Uncertainty / maturity

This is an evidence-backed strategic signal from one article and several reported enterprise examples, not a quantified cross-industry study. The direction of reallocation is plausible but not yet proven as a broad market-wide pattern.
