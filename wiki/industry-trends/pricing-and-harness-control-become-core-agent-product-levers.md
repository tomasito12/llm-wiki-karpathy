# Pricing and harness control are becoming core agent product levers

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
For coding agents, vendors are competing on more than model quality. Subscription metering, API-credit allocation, and control over the execution harness shape who can use which workflows economically and under what constraints.
