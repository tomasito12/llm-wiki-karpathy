# Inference demand overtakes training as the practical bottleneck

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
AI system planning shifts from one-time model training toward ongoing inference capacity, because deployed usage, tool calls, and long-running agent loops consume more compute over time. The operational consequence is that inference cost, utilization, and capacity allocation become central product constraints.

## Supporting Data Points

- "inference compute is a strategic resource, currently undervalued"
- "To a significant degree, we have to become an AI inference company now."
- "the computing demand of the work has gone up by 10,000 times"
- Intel CEO notes on rising CPU compute demand

Anchored to 2026-04-30; this is a medium-term observation that reflects serving pressure and capacity planning priorities as of late April 2026.
