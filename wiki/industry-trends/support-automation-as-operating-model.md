# Support Automation as an Operating Model

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
Support automation is most effective when organizations redesign roles, knowledge processes, routing, and measurement around the automation layer rather than treating it as a standalone feature. The pattern includes dedicated ownership for knowledge, conversation design, and continuous improvement.

## Supporting Data Points

- Fin reportedly resolves over 81% of support volume.
- Intercom reports a 300%+ increase in demand absorbed without proportional headcount growth.
- The company says it created dedicated roles such as Knowledge Manager and Conversation Designer.

## Time sensitivity

Actionable as of 2026-03-13; the source describes a mature vendor case study and the pattern may remain useful for teams planning support automation beyond that date, but the exact metrics are organization-specific.

## Uncertainty / maturity

This is based on one vendor-authored case study, so the evidence supports the operating pattern but not a general causal claim that the same redesign will produce the same outcomes elsewhere.
