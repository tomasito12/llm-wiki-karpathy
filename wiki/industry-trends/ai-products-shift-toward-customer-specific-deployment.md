# AI Products Shift Toward Customer-Specific Deployment

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
AI delivery is moving toward systems that are installed around a customer's own workflows, data layers, and operational constraints rather than delivered as generic standalone products. The practical implication is that deployment skill, customer immersion, and integration design matter more than a model demo. This trend is strongest where workflows are complex, legacy-heavy, or sensitive to business context.

## Supporting Data Points

- "being on-site with a customer"
- "building APIs over an existing data layer"
- "create an execution environment to test the agent safely"

## Time sensitivity

Actionable as of 2026-05-20; this appears relevant for enterprise AI delivery motions that need to integrate with real customer infrastructure and may remain so as long as enterprise workflows stay heterogeneous.

## Uncertainty / maturity

The evidence is opinionated and based on one practitioner account, so it does not prove the trend is universal or highest ROI across all deployments.
