# AI-Native Delivery Methodology

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
An AI-native delivery methodology is a software delivery approach where AI is embedded across the full work cycle instead of added only as a coding aid. It treats requirements, planning, business analysis, coordination, engineering, and deployment as parts of one AI-assisted workflow. The practical point is that once AI speeds up one stage, surrounding stages often become the real bottleneck. The durable operational lesson is to redesign the whole process, not just automate a single task.

## Examples

Endava created a named methodology, DavaFlow, and used it across “meeting preparation and business planning to product discovery, software engineering, and deployment.”

## Key Points

- AI should be integrated into the delivery lifecycle, not isolated as a developer helper.
- Once coding is accelerated, requirements, planning, and stakeholder coordination can become the limiting steps.
- A named methodology can help standardize AI usage across multiple functions and teams.

## Operational Insight

When AI is adopted inside delivery teams, the highest leverage comes from reworking adjacent human steps, not from isolated prompt use in one function.
