# Hybrid Human-AI Workflows for Expert Review

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
The most durable automation gains often come from workflows that use AI to support human judgment rather than replace it. In these designs, AI handles parts of the task that benefit from speed or scale, while people retain responsibility for review, edge cases, and final decisions. The practical design question is how to allocate work so AI lifts capacity without removing the expert checks that preserve quality. This pattern is important in domains where throughput matters, but the cost of a bad decision is high.

## Examples

The article states that "The most durable gains came from hybrid workflows—using AI to lift the ceiling on expert reasoning and review, not just increase throughput."

## Key Points

- AI can raise throughput while humans retain final judgment.
- Hybrid workflows are more durable when the task has meaningful edge cases or quality risk.
- The design target is augmented expert review, not throughput alone.
