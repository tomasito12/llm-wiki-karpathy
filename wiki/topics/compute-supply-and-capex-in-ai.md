# Compute Supply and Capex in AI

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
AI systems at frontier scale are constrained not only by model quality but by the physical and financial capacity to build and power them. When demand rises quickly, the limiting factors become chips, data centers, energy, construction, and balance-sheet structure. In practice, the engineering problem expands into a deployment and capital-allocation problem. Teams need to think about supply chains, build rates, and financing as part of system design.

## Key Points

- Frontier AI scaling creates a capital-deployment problem, not just an ML optimization problem.
- Physical bottlenecks can delay capacity even when demand is strong.
- Balance-sheet structure becomes part of the deployment stack when capex rises sharply.

## Operational Insight

For large AI deployments, treat compute procurement and construction capacity as first-class planning inputs, not downstream implementation details. The practical question is whether demand can be converted into usable capacity faster than the surrounding infrastructure can absorb capital.
