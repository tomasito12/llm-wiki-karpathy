# Structured Drafting for Rereadable Notes

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
Rereadable notes are not just compressed summaries; they are shaped artifacts that preserve the writer's judgment about what matters. The durable knowledge is that note quality improves when the author actively selects, rewrites, and orders the material for future reading. This makes the note itself more than a record: it becomes a reusable interpretation. The approach is strongest when the content will be revisited in a bounded context rather than mined mechanically.

## Examples

The source contrasts "bullet point lists" and "AI-generated summaries" with "prose I had shaped myself" that could be read in a store while making decisions.

## Key Points

- Notes meant for rereading benefit from authorial shaping, not just compression.
- Sentence order and wording can matter because they preserve the reasoning path behind a decision.
- A distilled note can function as a retrieval aid only if it still carries enough of the original judgment to be trusted.

## Operational Insight

When notes are intended for future judgment and recall, favor human-shaped prose over mechanically compressed summaries. The selection and wording process are part of the value, not just the output format.
