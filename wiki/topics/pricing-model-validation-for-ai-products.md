# Pricing Model Validation for AI Products

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
Pricing model validation is the process of testing whether a monetization structure fits how buyers think about value before committing to a price point. It combines qualitative research on buyer expectations with structured willingness-to-pay studies and then checks the result against business constraints such as margins, discounting, and adoption. This reduces the risk of optimizing for a number that looks good in a spreadsheet but does not match market behavior. The method is especially useful when a product category is new enough that buyers do not have stable expectations about the right commercial structure.

## Key Points

- Qualitative research should establish what buyers consider valuable.
- Willingness-to-pay studies are stronger when they test a clear pricing model and metric.
- Commercial modeling should incorporate discounting, segment differences, and margins.
- Intent data needs a second pass before it is treated as a forecast.

## Operational Insight

Use willingness-to-pay methods only after the model and metric are settled. Otherwise the survey may answer the wrong question with convincing precision.
