# Enterprise AI Layer

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A shared enterprise AI layer is a secure internal AI environment that different teams can reuse for distinct workflows instead of building isolated point solutions. It works best when access, governance, monitoring, and evaluation are designed alongside the tooling so new use cases can be deployed quickly without fragmenting controls. The pattern is especially useful in regulated organizations where clinical, operational, and research users need different permissions but common infrastructure. Its value comes from making AI a shared platform for repeated work, not a one-off assistant.

## Examples

A hospital described a "secure internal ChatGPT environment" used across "research, clinical, and administrative teams" with governance built alongside the technology.

## Key Points

- A fragmented one-off approach can expose limits once multiple teams need AI.
- Governance, safety, monitoring, and evaluation need to be part of the platform, not added later.
- A shared layer can reduce deployment cycles from long custom projects to days when the foundation is in place.

## Operational Insight

Treat the AI layer as internal platform infrastructure: centralize access and governance, then let teams ship role-specific workflows on top of it.
