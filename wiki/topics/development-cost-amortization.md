# Development Cost Amortization

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
Development cost amortization in AI refers to spreading research, experimentation, and infrastructure learnings across many future iterations instead of paying those costs repeatedly inside each lab. When the expensive work is mostly R&D, shared documentation and cross-team learning can matter as much as the final trained model. This changes the unit of advantage from a single training run to the accumulated body of methods, reports, and infrastructure know-how. The concept is especially relevant where systems are iterated rapidly and many teams converge on similar technical problems.

## Key Points

- If frontier compute is mostly R&D, avoiding duplicated research can materially change burn rate.
- Public documentation can function as a shared cost-reduction layer across labs.
- The benefit is ecosystem-wide, but the immediate effect still accrues to future development, not to inference buyers.
- The size of the savings is uncertain without full methodology and cost accounting.

## Operational Insight

Look for reuse of research and infra knowledge as a real cost lever. If the same experimental steps are being repeated inside multiple teams, the ecosystem is leaving money on the table.
