# AI Governance as Product Surface

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
Governance becomes a product concern when systems are deployed widely enough that decisions about permissions, privacy, and acceptable use shape what the product can safely do. In that stage, governance is not only a legal or compliance layer; it is part of how the system is designed, restricted, and trusted in production. The practical work includes deciding which tasks AI may handle, what data it may access, and where human oversight remains necessary.

## Key Points

- Governance can determine the allowable scope of AI features, not just their compliance posture.
- Privacy and ethics become operational design inputs when AI touches real user workflows.
- Human oversight remains a core system property in regulated or sensitive deployments.

## Operational Insight

The source argues that governance, privacy, and ethics are among the most valuable roles as AI spreads. That is a durable reminder that product teams need to design for boundaries and oversight, not just capability expansion.
