# Workflow Restructuring Around AI Agents

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
AI value increases when teams redesign end-to-end workflows instead of only adding a chatbot to an existing task. The important shift is from isolated task acceleration to changing how work is routed, reviewed, and handed off across functions. This usually requires pairing model access with governance, integration into existing tools, and visible early wins that create adoption momentum. The pattern matters most in organizations where knowledge work is fragmented and governed by compliance or quality constraints.

## Key Points

- The highest leverage comes from changing workflow structure, not just task execution.
- Adoption improves when AI appears inside environments people already use.
- Governance and human review can be built into the workflow from the start without blocking scale.

## Operational Insight

Treat generative AI as a workflow redesign project: identify a high-friction process, expose the model inside the team’s existing working surface, and add explicit review gates for outputs that affect customers or regulated decisions.
