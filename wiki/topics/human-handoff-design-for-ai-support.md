# Human Handoff Design for AI Support

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
When an AI system handles a large share of service conversations, the handoff to a human becomes a core product experience rather than a fallback. Good handoff design routes the user to the right person, preserves context, and minimizes friction during escalation. That means conversation design must consider the entire journey, not only the AI's standalone answer quality. Routing, skill assignment, and escalation logic are part of the service design layer.

## Key Points

- Skills-based routing can reduce the cost of escalation by sending the case directly to the right specialist.
- Conversation design should include the human end of the journey, not only the automated response.
- The handoff layer becomes more important as AI coverage increases and edge cases move to humans.

## Operational Insight

Do not treat escalation as an afterthought; build routing and conversation design as part of the automation stack so users move cleanly from AI to the right human.
