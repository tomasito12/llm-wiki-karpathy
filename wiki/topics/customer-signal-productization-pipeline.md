# Customer Signal Productization Pipeline

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A durable operating pattern for converting high-touch customer work into reusable product capabilities. The flow is: learn from bespoke engagements, standardize the useful analysis into internal tooling, and only then promote patterns into customer-facing product features. This requires an explicit generalization gate, because insights that help one account or one team may not be robust enough for broad release. The pattern is especially relevant when early customer success work produces valuable signals that are too manual to scale directly.

## Key Points

- High-touch customer work can surface better signals than large-scale generic analysis.
- Internal tooling can convert one-off analyses into repeatable workflows for non-specialists.
- A feature should move into the product only after it is broadly useful, robust, and scalable without configuration.

## Operational Insight

Treat customer-facing expert work as an input to product discovery, but do not ship it until it has been encoded into repeatable analysis and tested across a wider set of accounts.
