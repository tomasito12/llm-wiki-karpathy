# Consumer AI Monetization Under Load

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
High-usage consumer AI creates an economic problem: token growth, compute cost, and user expectations can outpace simple subscription pricing. Providers then have to combine efficiency gains, usage caps, and ad-supported surfaces to keep the service viable. This is not just a billing issue; it shapes product design, access policy, and the kinds of tasks the system can afford to support. When usage becomes massive, monetization strategy turns into infrastructure strategy. The practical outcome is that product generosity often has hard limits at scale.

## Key Points

- Usage caps can be a product lever as well as a cost-control lever.
- Ad-supported AI responses are a plausible monetization path when direct billing is insufficient.
- Token growth is a practical proxy for infrastructure strain, though it does not by itself reveal unit economics.

## Operational Insight

When consumer AI usage becomes large enough, pricing, caps, and ads stop being side issues and become core architecture choices.
