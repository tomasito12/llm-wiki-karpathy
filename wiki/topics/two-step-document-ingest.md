# Two-Step Document Ingest

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A safer document-ingest workflow separates analysis from writing. First the system reads the source and extracts entities, claims, contradictions, and candidate destinations; only then does it generate or update durable knowledge. This reduces the risk that incomplete, outdated, or ambiguous source material gets converted into permanent guidance. The pattern is especially useful when sources mix drafts, changing assumptions, and operational decisions.

## Key Points

- Separate source interpretation from page generation.
- Identify contradictions and review items before writing durable pages.
- Treat drafts and outdated assumptions as provisional, not canonical.
- Use staged ingest to reduce false certainty in knowledge bases.

## Operational Insight

Use a staging analysis pass to decide what is factual, what is provisional, and what needs human review before any wiki pages are updated. This creates a check against false certainty and makes ingestion easier to trust.
