# Human Feedback Loops in Support Operations

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
Support organizations can turn frontline case handling into a structured improvement channel. Agents and support reps are well positioned to identify repeat issues, explain why a case escalated, and propose fixes to content, process, or tooling. The key design requirement is to keep feedback capture lightweight so it happens in the flow of work. This pattern connects service delivery with product and knowledge-base maintenance.

## Examples

The source describes reps asking, "Why did this reach me? Why couldn’t Fin resolve it? How can we prevent this from happening again?" and logging suggestions "in the moment" with minimal admin.

## Key Points

- Frontline reps can identify failure modes that dashboards miss.
- Fast review and implementation loops prevent suggestion backlogs.
- The pattern links human escalation with knowledge-base and tooling updates.
- Minimal logging friction is a prerequisite for participation.

## Operational Insight

If the people closest to customers are not given a cheap way to record recurring problems, the organization loses its best signal for reducing repeat work. The durable design pattern is to make case review a source of product and knowledge updates.
