# Prompts as Small Workflows

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A prompt can function as a reusable workflow when it bakes in multiple steps such as explanation, testing, correction, planning, or validation. Instead of producing a single static answer, the model is asked to act as a loop or sequence that moves the user toward a better outcome. This turns prompting into a lightweight orchestration technique.

## Key Points

- A useful prompt can combine explanation, questions, correction, and next-step guidance.
- The article frames prompt design as a system design problem, not a phrasing problem.
- Prompt chaining is presented as a way to separate draft, critique, and revision steps.

## Operational Insight

Treat prompts as reusable procedures when you need learning, building, or decision support. Multi-step prompts are more durable than one-shot prompts because they encode the desired interaction pattern, not just the requested content.
