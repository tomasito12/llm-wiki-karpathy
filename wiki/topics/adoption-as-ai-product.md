# Adoption as AI Product

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
Treating AI rollout as the product means measuring usage, shaping behavior change, and managing governance as first-class parts of deployment. The focus shifts from proving a model in a pilot to getting a workforce to use AI safely, consistently, and at scale. This approach usually depends on workflow-specific training, visible metrics, and local champions rather than only central enablement. It is most useful where the hard problem is not model capability but organizational uptake.

## Examples

AdventHealth leadership said, “We made a decision early on to treat adoption as the product.” They also tracked “messages per user per business day” as a KPI.

## Key Points

- Usage can be tracked as a KPI, not just a byproduct of deployment.
- Domain-based peer groups can spread practical workflow patterns faster than one-size-fits-all training.
- Treating adoption as the product reframes AI from automation hype into managed organizational change.
- Governance and safety become part of the operating model when usage is scaled across a regulated workforce.

## Operational Insight

For enterprise AI, design the rollout plan, usage metrics, and change-management loop as part of the system, not as afterthoughts.
