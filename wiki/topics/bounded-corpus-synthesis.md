# Bounded Corpus Synthesis

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A bounded corpus is a deliberately scoped set of documents that shares a clear center of gravity, such as one project, one domain, or one workflow. AI synthesis works better when the corpus is coherent, because the system can create meaningful structure instead of merely generating links across unrelated clutter. When the input set is too broad or miscellaneous, the output may still be fluent, but the knowledge artifact loses interpretability and practical value. Scope discipline is therefore a core design constraint for durable AI-assisted knowledge work.

## Key Points

- A coherent corpus gives the wiki a shared center of gravity.
- Unrelated notes can be summarized, but they do not automatically form meaningful knowledge.
- Scope should be chosen around a project, domain, book, or workflow rather than an entire life archive.
- Meaningful synthesis depends on selection quality before model quality.

## Operational Insight

Choose a narrow, coherent corpus before introducing AI-assisted compilation. The quality of the maintained knowledge artifact depends more on topical coherence than on the model's summarization ability.
