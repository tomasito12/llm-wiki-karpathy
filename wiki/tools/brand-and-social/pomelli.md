## What problem does this tool solve?

Generate **on-brand social posts** from a **website URL**—extracting palette, fonts, tone, and messaging into a profile, then outputting platform-formatted variants.

## Properties

- Site (author): [pomelli.me](https://pomelli.me).
- Author: **Google Labs** product; **free beta** at time of writing (verify).
- Author claims **~10 post variations in ~60 seconds** and **30–40% faster than Canva** workflows in “independent tests” (**unverified** here).
