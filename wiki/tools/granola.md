# Granola

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A meeting-notes app that combines automatic transcription with manual notes taken during the call. The article positions it as a structured summary tool rather than a raw transcript dump.

## Core Capabilities

- It combines automatic transcript generation with user-written notes to produce a more structured meeting record.
- It supports custom output templates, which helps adapt the same tool to different reporting formats.
- It connects to major meeting platforms, making it easy to fit into standard remote-work setups.

## Integration Ecosystem

- The article lists Google Meet, Zoom, and Teams as supported platforms.
- It is framed as a meeting-notes app rather than a standalone recorder, so it fits into existing conferencing workflows.

## Maturity signals

The availability of a free basic plan suggests low-friction adoption. The product is described as distinct from other transcription tools by virtue of its workflow design rather than raw transcription alone. The source is mostly descriptive, so maturity signals should be treated as practical but not deeply validated.

## Strengths

- It merges transcript text with the user’s own notes, which helps preserve both spoken content and what the participant considered important.
- Custom output templates make it easier to standardize meeting notes for different clients, teams, or recurring call types.
- Support for Google Meet, Zoom, and Teams makes it compatible with the most common meeting environments.

## Weaknesses / limitations

The source does not explain note accuracy, speaker attribution quality, or how it handles noisy meetings. It also does not show whether template customization is flexible enough for complex organizational workflows. The value is strong for note-taking, but the article provides limited evidence about broader collaboration or automation use.
