## What problem does this tool solve?

**Operational coordination and documentation**—the source positions ChatGPT as helping ops teams structure fragmented inputs into updates, **SOPs**, **decision logs**, **handoffs**, and **incident** artifacts so people spend less time re-explaining context.

## Properties

- Product framing from **OpenAI Academy** (Apr 2026); primary URL cited in capture: [Operations guide](https://openai.com/academy/operations).
- **Use-case table** in source spans: operating cadence & reporting; process & handoffs; incident & escalation; vendor & partner ops; capacity & planning; metrics & data hygiene—each with example **outputs** (exec summaries, RACI drafts, postmortem outlines, vendor scorecards, capacity scenarios, KPI QA checklists).
- **Named product capabilities** in source (with Academy links): **Projects** (multi-step work over time), **Skills** (repeatable workflows), **Data analysis**, **Deep research**, **Image generation** (diagrams / rollout visuals)—verify availability for your workspace/tenant.
- Source stresses providing **goal, stakeholders, timeline, constraints, and real inputs** (docs, trackers, meeting outputs) for best results.
