## What problem does this tool solve?

**System-wide voice dictation** that turns speech into clean text in existing apps—aimed at people who type all day.

## Properties

- Site (author): [wisprflow.ai](https://wisprflow.ai).
- Author claims **~500 ms latency**, works across **Gmail, Notion, Claude, Twitter**, etc.; **100+ languages**; **SOC 2 Type II** for teams—verify on vendor.
- Author cites early-2026 reviewers calling it the most accurate dictation on any OS (**third-party claim relayed without links**).
