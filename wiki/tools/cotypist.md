# Cotypist

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A context-aware autocompleter for macOS that uses local screen snapshots to infer what you are working on.

## Core Capabilities

- It suggests text based on what is visible on the screen, which can cut down on repeated manual typing.
- It operates locally so the source claims your screen snapshots do not leave the Mac.

## Maturity signals

The app is presented as a polished utility rather than an experiment, but the source provides no adoption numbers or enterprise signals. Its niche is clear: keyboard-level productivity for Mac users who want context-aware typing without sending data off device. As of 2026-04-24, it reads as an appealing point solution rather than a proven platform.

## Strengths

- Uses local screen context to make completions feel more relevant than generic autocomplete, which can reduce repetitive typing in real workflows.
- Keeps snapshots on the device, which lowers privacy concern compared with cloud-based context capture.
- Fits naturally into email and project communication workflows where names, clients, and jargon repeat often.

## Weaknesses / limitations

The source does not explain how accurate it is across different apps, how much screen content it stores, or how it behaves when context is ambiguous. There is no evidence here about battery impact, latency under load, or whether the local approach fails on complex tasks.
