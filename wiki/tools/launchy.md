# Launchy

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A lightweight app switcher for macOS that shows a small circular launcher near the cursor with a limited set of apps. It is designed for fast switching with less visual clutter.

## Core Capabilities

- It presents a small launcher at the cursor for fast app switching.
- It intentionally limits the visible app set to keep the interaction simple.

## Maturity signals

The source frames it as a simpler alternative rather than a flagship platform. As of 2025-12-31, it appears to be a niche utility with no maturity evidence beyond the author's preference.

## Strengths

- Keeps the launcher small and cursor-adjacent, which reduces movement and makes the switch action feel immediate.
- Limits the chooser to six apps, which can improve decision speed for a small core set of tools.
- Prioritizes simplicity over breadth, which aligns with a low-friction desktop workflow.

## Weaknesses / limitations

The limitation is also the tradeoff: six apps may be too few for users with broader switching needs. The article does not provide details on customization depth or compatibility.
