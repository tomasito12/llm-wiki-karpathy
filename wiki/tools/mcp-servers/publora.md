## What problem does this tool solve?

Publishing and scheduling **social posts across many networks** from a single MCP-driven workflow instead of juggling multiple dashboards.

## Properties

- Described as an **MCP server** with **11+** platforms named: Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and “more.”
- Onboarding framed as under **two minutes** to connect to an MCP client; example natural-language scheduling via Claude.
- Positioned for **indie builders and content creators** in-source.
- Link: [publora.com](https://publora.com/).
