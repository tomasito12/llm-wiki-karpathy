# iA Writer

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A distraction-free markdown writing app for macOS with no collaboration layer or AI editing. The author uses it for drafts, docs, and journaling because it stays close to plain text.

## Core Capabilities

- It provides a plain markdown editor for writing without formatting clutter.
- It keeps AI out of the editing layer, which the author prefers for focused drafting.
- It supports personal and work writing in the same lightweight environment.

## Integration Ecosystem

- It stores files in a way that pairs with iCloud-based plain markdown workflows.
- The source notes separate cross-platform versions are sold for iPad, iPhone, Windows, and Android.

## Maturity signals

The author’s repeated use across multiple writing contexts suggests a stable, durable fit rather than an experimental choice. The public commitment to remain AI-free at the editing layer is a clear product stance that the source treats as dependable. No ecosystem breadth is claimed beyond Mac and separately sold platform versions.

## Strengths

- Plain markdown keeps the file format portable and simple.
- The absence of formatting chrome reduces distraction during long writing sessions.
- The app is explicitly AI-free at the editing layer, which the author treats as a feature rather than a gap.
- It supports the author’s use cases across drafts, work docs, a personal journal, and article ideas, which shows broad writing utility within a single-user workflow.

## Weaknesses / limitations

The source is explicit about what it does not do: collaboration, rich text, and AI features. That makes it a poor fit for team editing or workflows that expect inline AI assistance. The article also implies the value is largely preference-based, not category-leading by objective benchmark.
