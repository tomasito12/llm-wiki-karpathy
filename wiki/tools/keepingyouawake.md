# KeepingYouAwake

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A menu bar toggle that prevents a Mac from sleeping. It is meant for long downloads, presentations, or other tasks where sleep would interrupt work.

## Core Capabilities

- It keeps the Mac awake with a simple menu bar toggle.
- It lets users reverse the setting immediately when the task is done.

## Maturity signals

The source portrays it as a no-frills utility rather than a complex power-management suite. As of 2025-12-31, no broader adoption or maintenance signal is provided.

## Strengths

- Adds a single menu bar toggle, which makes sleep prevention easy to start and stop.
- Avoids settings hunting and timer setup, so it keeps the interaction fast and explicit.
- Fits scenarios like downloads, presentations, or hands-free reading, which are common practical reasons to keep a laptop awake.

## Weaknesses / limitations

The article does not mention whether it handles scheduled durations, battery safeguards, or enterprise policies. It solves one narrow problem, so its value is limited to the moments when sleep is the issue.
