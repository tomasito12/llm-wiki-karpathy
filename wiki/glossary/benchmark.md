# Benchmark

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A benchmark is a standardized test used to measure the performance of a system, model, or tool against a defined task or set of tasks. In AI, benchmarks help compare capabilities across models and versions, though they do not always predict real-world usefulness.

## Relevance Note

Benchmarks shape model selection, vendor comparisons, and internal release gates. They are especially important when the benchmark maps to operational tasks like support, retrieval, coding, or multi-step workflow completion.
