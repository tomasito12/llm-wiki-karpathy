# World model

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
A world model is an internal representation of how a system changes over time. It predicts future states, effects of actions, and relationships between objects or events, rather than only generating language.

## Relevance Note

World models matter for robotics, autonomous agents, planning systems, and simulation-heavy workflows because they provide a way to test actions before execution. They are especially relevant when the cost of a bad action is high or when the environment changes over time.
