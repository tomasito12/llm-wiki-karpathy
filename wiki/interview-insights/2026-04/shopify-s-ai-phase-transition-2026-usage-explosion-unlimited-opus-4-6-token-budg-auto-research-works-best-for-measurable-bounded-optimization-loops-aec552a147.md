# Auto-research works best for measurable, bounded optimization loops

## Interview Insight

### Summary

Tangent is described as an auto-research loop that can run experiments, modify them, and optimize a goal inside Tangle. Parakhin says it is highly effective for obvious improvements, search optimization, prompt compression, storage reduction, and similar measurable tasks, and that it is democratizing for PMs and domain experts. He also says it is weak on problems that are truly out of distribution or require several days of original reasoning.

### Why It Matters

Actionable as of 2026-04-22 because it clarifies where auto-experimentation is strong enough to delegate and where it is not. The useful boundary is durable: the best use cases are objective-heavy, measurable, and already instrumented. That is more valuable than a generic claim that AutoML or agents can do everything.

### Operational Relevance

Use auto-research loops when the objective is explicit, the metric is measurable, and repeated experiment runs are cheap enough to automate. Keep humans in the loop for novel problem framing and out-of-distribution changes; do not expect the loop to invent unfamiliar strategies reliably.

### Service Automation Relevance

Indirect relevance: the same bounded-optimization pattern can improve support flows when objectives are measurable, but it will not replace human judgment for ambiguous cases.
