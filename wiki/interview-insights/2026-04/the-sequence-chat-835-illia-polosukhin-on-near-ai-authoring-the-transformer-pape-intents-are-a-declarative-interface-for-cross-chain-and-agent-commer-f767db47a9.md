# Intents are a declarative interface for cross-chain and agent commerce

## Interview Insight

### Summary

NEAR Intents is described as a system where a user declares a desired outcome with constraints, and matching logic finds someone who can fulfill it at the right cost and requirements. Polosukhin says this began with crypto-to-crypto transfers, expanded into wallet interactions like CrossPay, and now extends to Agent Market for natural-language work requests. The reusable idea is that intent-based interfaces compress complex multi-step commerce into outcome declarations and matching.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-04-02, this is a durable pattern for agent-native commerce and payments. It suggests a product shape where users specify outcomes instead of procedures, and where matching, settlement, and fulfillment are pushed into infrastructure.

### Operational Relevance

For workflow and payment design, treat intents as a declarative abstraction over routing, matching, and settlement. This can simplify cross-chain interactions, agent marketplaces, and task fulfillment systems by replacing imperative steps with constraint-based requests.

### Service Automation Relevance

Relevant to support and commerce automation because it frames requests as outcomes rather than scripts. That can reduce user friction in complex service flows where an assistant needs to coordinate multiple systems, assets, or counterparties.
