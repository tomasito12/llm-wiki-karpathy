# Physical AI bottlenecks are deployment and verification, not just model quality

## Interview Insight

### Summary

The founders argue that the limiting factor in physical AI is often getting a model to run safely on constrained hardware, not increasing raw intelligence. They emphasize latency, power, reliability, fail-safes, updates, and human validation as the hard parts of production deployment. In their framing, better models help, but systems work is what makes them usable on real machines.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-04-27, this is a durable reminder that autonomy teams should budget for embedded constraints and verification from the start, not treat them as downstream implementation details. It reframes physical AI as a systems integration problem with safety constraints, which changes team structure, milestone planning, and risk assessment.

### Operational Relevance

Build plans should include embedded performance budgets, redundancy, fallbacks, and validation gates alongside model development. Teams should expect to compress and distill models for onboard execution and to treat human review as mandatory in safety-critical paths.
