# Autonomy evaluation is shifting from binary tests to statistical reliability

## Interview Insight

### Summary

The interview contrasts older vehicle validation regimes with the newer reality of probabilistic model behavior. Instead of asking whether a system passes a fixed test, the question becomes how many nines of reliability it achieves and what its mean time between failures looks like. The founders also frame regulator education as part of the deployment process, not a separate afterthought.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-04-27, this is a high-value mental model for autonomy governance because it maps AI progress onto statistical safety rather than one-shot pass/fail certification. That matters for teams that need to communicate reliability to customers, regulators, and internal safety reviewers.

### Operational Relevance

Evaluation plans should define reliability targets, failure-rate measurement, and scenario coverage instead of relying only on checklist tests. Teams should prepare to explain statistical confidence in plain language to non-technical stakeholders.
