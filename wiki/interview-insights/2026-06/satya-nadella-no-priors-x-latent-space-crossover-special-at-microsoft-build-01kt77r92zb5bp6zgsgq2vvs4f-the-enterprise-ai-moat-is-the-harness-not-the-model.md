# The enterprise AI moat is the harness, not the model

## Interview Insight

### Summary

Nadella argues that the durable layer is the system around the model: context, tools, data, traces, and private evals. A model can be swapped, but if the organization cannot preserve performance across model changes, it does not control the workflow. This makes the harness the real unit of design for enterprise AI.

### Why It Matters

Actionable as of 2026-06-03: teams should invest in controllable systems, not just model selection. The source treats harness quality as a durable differentiator for AI engineering and enterprise adoption, especially where workflows need repeatability and switching leverage.

### Operational Relevance

Design for multi-model harnesses, progressive tool access, and rich context layers. Build eval loops that let a company compare models against private criteria and improve a specific workflow over time. Treat the orchestration layer as the place where control, portability, and reliability are created.

### Service Automation Relevance

Strong: support and back-office automation will depend on whether the system can carry context, delegate tools safely, and preserve traceable state across handoffs. The transcript suggests that better service automation comes from harness design, not from a better chatbot surface alone.
