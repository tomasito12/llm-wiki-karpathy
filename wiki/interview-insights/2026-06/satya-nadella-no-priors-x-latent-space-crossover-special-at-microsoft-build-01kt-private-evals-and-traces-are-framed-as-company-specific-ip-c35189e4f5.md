# Private evals and traces are framed as company-specific IP

## Interview Insight

### Summary

Nadella repeatedly says public benchmarks are not enough because real value is measured on a company's own work. He argues that private evals, traces, and the ability to hill-climb from Model A to Model B without losing performance are a source of control and IP. The key test is whether the system can improve on private criteria without leaking traces.

### Why It Matters

Actionable as of 2026-06-03: teams building enterprise AI should treat private eval design as a core asset, not a side task. This is a durable evaluation pattern for AI engineering because it ties model choice to workflow control and switching ability.

### Operational Relevance

Build private eval sets from real tasks, keep traces internal, and verify that model swaps preserve or improve outcomes. Use these evals to choose between providers and to detect when a harness is overfit to generic benchmarks. The source suggests that eval infrastructure becomes part of the product moat.

### Service Automation Relevance

For service automation, private evals are the mechanism for judging whether a chatbot or agent truly handles the company's cases, policies, and handoff rules. Without them, support automation can look good in demos but fail on live tickets and internal exceptions.
