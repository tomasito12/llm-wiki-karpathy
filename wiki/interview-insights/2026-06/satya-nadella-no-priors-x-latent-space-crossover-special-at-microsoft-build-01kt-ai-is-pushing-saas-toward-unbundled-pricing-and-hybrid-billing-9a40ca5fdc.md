# AI is pushing SaaS toward unbundled pricing and hybrid billing

## Interview Insight

### Summary

The transcript says SaaS business models are being re-litigated because agentic usage changes cost structure and customer expectations. Nadella expects per-user subscriptions to remain, but alongside consumption pricing and, in some cases, outcomes-based pricing. He also says vendors need flexibility because different buyers will prefer different billing shapes.

### Why It Matters

Actionable as of 2026-06-03: product and finance teams need to design multiple billing modes instead of assuming one default SaaS price model. The source is useful because it ties pricing to budget certainty, usage intensity, and the economics of agent workloads.

### Operational Relevance

If a product moves from interactive use to long-running agents, per-user pricing may underprice usage and mask cost. Teams should prepare for consumption meters and test whether outcome-based pricing creates customer resistance once the tradeoff becomes clear. This is especially relevant for enterprise AI products with variable agent runtime.

### Service Automation Relevance

Support automation products may need usage-based pricing when agents run continuously or handle many delegated tasks. The billing model should reflect actual automation load rather than only named seats.
