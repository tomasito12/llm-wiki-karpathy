# Agent infrastructure sales are won by responsiveness and trust, not just benchmarks

## Interview Insight

### Summary

Burazin says the strongest reason customers stay or return is Daytona’s responsiveness: fast replies in Slack, quick huddles, and direct support from the team. He explicitly downplays GitHub stars and says benchmarks are table stakes rather than the main buying criterion. The operational lesson is that infrastructure products selling into production workflows are evaluated on support quality and trust as much as on raw performance.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-05-21, this is a durable go-to-market lesson for AI infrastructure vendors. In production agent systems, support latency can be part of the product because customers need rapid help when workflows break. The interview suggests that operational closeness can be a real differentiator even when the technical market is crowded.

### Operational Relevance

If you sell runtime infrastructure, invest in live support channels, fast escalation, and high-trust customer relationships. Do not assume benchmarks alone will convert teams using agent sandboxes in production. Operational responsiveness is part of the product surface.

### Service Automation Relevance

For service automation platforms, reliability and response speed can matter more than demo polish when customers are handling live workflows. Human-in-the-loop support and fast escalation paths can reduce adoption friction in enterprise automation.
