# Open source improves trust and context more than direct conversion

## Interview Insight

### Summary

Burazin says Daytona’s open-source posture helps because customers can inspect the code, send the repository to their agent, and gain more integration context. But he also says it has not been a major direct growth engine by itself. In other words, openness is framed as a trust and comprehension layer, not a standalone acquisition strategy.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-05-21, this is a useful operating lesson for infrastructure vendors. Open source can reduce adoption friction and improve technical credibility, but it should not be assumed to convert self-hosters or drive growth automatically without a strong product and distribution loop.

### Operational Relevance

For agent infrastructure teams, repository transparency can help agents and engineers understand behavior, but the commercial path still depends on cloud consumption, support, and workflow fit. This argues for treating open source as an enablement layer rather than the whole go-to-market plan.

### Service Automation Relevance

For support automation buyers, open source can increase trust in sensitive infrastructure, especially when agents need access to internal systems. However, the source suggests that trust alone does not guarantee adoption if the product fit is weak.
