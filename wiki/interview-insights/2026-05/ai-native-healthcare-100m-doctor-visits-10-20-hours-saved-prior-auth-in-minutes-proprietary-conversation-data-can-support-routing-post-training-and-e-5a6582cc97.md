# Proprietary conversation data can support routing, post-training, and efficiency work

## Interview Insight

### Summary

Abridge says its unique asset is a very large corpus of medical conversations, which functions like execution traces for healthcare. That data supports better note generation, diarization, and future agent training, while also making model routing and efficiency optimization more valuable at scale. The interview explicitly ties scale to post-training, token efficiency, and cost control rather than simply using the biggest available model everywhere.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-05-14, this is a durable infrastructure pattern: when a product owns domain traces, it can use them to improve quality, reduce latency, and lower cost in ways generic providers cannot. That matters for any AI application that eventually reaches high volume and needs a differentiated operating cost structure.

### Operational Relevance

Build data flywheels around real workflow traces, then use that data to choose where to route to smaller or larger models. Once volume is high, optimize for compute and tokens in the highest-volume paths rather than relying on frontier models for every step.

### Service Automation Relevance

For support systems, conversation logs and resolution traces can become the training and routing substrate for faster, cheaper, more reliable automation. The same architecture applies to handoff detection, response drafting, and issue classification.
