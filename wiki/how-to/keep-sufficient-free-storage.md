# Keep Sufficient Free Storage

## Current understanding

<!-- stage1-placeholder: single-source lead; Stage 2 will synthesize from accumulated EvidenceItems -->
macOS uses disk space as virtual memory when RAM is under pressure. That means storage is part of performance, not just a place to keep files. The problem is that a nearly full drive can reduce how well the system handles heavy multitasking or large workloads. New users often overlook free space because they think only RAM matters.

## Caveats

The 50 gigabyte figure is presented as advice, not a measured threshold. Actual needs can vary with workload, disk size, and how much swap pressure the system sees.

## Implementation Steps

- Check available disk space regularly.
- Keep a meaningful amount of free space on the drive.
- Free space proactively before heavy workflows such as video editing or many open tabs.
- Treat storage pressure as a possible cause of sluggishness.

## Prerequisites

- A way to monitor disk usage
- Enough storage to leave headroom
