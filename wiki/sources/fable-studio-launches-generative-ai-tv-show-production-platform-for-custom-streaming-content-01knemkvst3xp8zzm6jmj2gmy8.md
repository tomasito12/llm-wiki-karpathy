# Fable Studio Launches Generative AI TV Show Production Platform for Custom Streaming Content

Fable Studio announced a new product called Showrunner. It is meant to let people make animated television episodes by typing in prompts and then changing things like dialogue, characters, and how scenes look. The company says the service includes ten AI-made shows in different styles, such as horror, satire, and anthology storytelling. People can sign up to join a waitlist, but the platform was not broadly open yet. Fable also said that users could create episodes for existing shows and that the best ones might be added to the official catalog. In some cases, creators of winning episodes could get paid and might share revenue if a streaming service picks up the show. The article also mentions that Fable had already gotten attention for AI-generated South Park episodes, which some people praised and others criticized. The main takeaway as of 2024-06-04 is that this was an early product launch with strong promotional framing, but not much proof yet about quality, scale, or business success.

## Key insights

- Showrunner is positioned as a prompt-to-episode system, not just a clip generator.
- Fable is testing a user-contribution model where audience-made episodes can enter the official catalog.
- The launch bundles content generation with a potential creator payout and revenue-share path.
- The source gives no operational metrics, so claims about production efficiency remain unproven as of 2024-06-04.
- The article’s strongest evidence is product positioning, not implementation detail.
