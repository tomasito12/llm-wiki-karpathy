# How Endava is redesigning software delivery around AI agents

This article is about how Endava changed its software delivery process by making AI part of everyday work. The company did not just give people new tools; it redesigned how teams gather requirements, plan work, build software, and coordinate across functions. OpenAI’s ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex sit inside that workflow. The interesting part is that the biggest gains were not limited to coding. Legal, project management, commercial planning, and leadership teams also started using AI for reports, research, and coordination. The core lesson is simple: if AI is treated as part of the process, not a side project, it can reshape how a services company operates.

## Key insights

- Endava’s main move was organizational: it treated AI adoption as workflow redesign, not as a software rollout.
- Once developers used AI-assisted coding and agents, non-coding bottlenecks such as requirements, planning, and stakeholder coordination became the limiting factor.
- DavaFlow is the company’s named delivery methodology for embedding OpenAI tools across the full lifecycle, from meeting prep to deployment.
- The article highlights practical, low-friction uses outside engineering, including legal research, governance reports, and lightweight internal apps built without dedicated engineering support.
- Endava ties adoption to leadership behavior and hiring/promotion expectations, which makes AI fluency part of the operating model rather than an optional skill.

## Why it matters

The article is useful because it shows a concrete enterprise pattern for making AI operational: put models and agents into the actual delivery process, then redesign the process around them. That is more durable than a one-off productivity experiment because Endava describes changes across requirements, planning, engineering, legal, project management, and commercial work. The named DavaFlow methodology is a reusable concept for wiki readers because it compresses the idea of an AI-native delivery lifecycle into one operational frame. The piece also makes a practical point that is easy to miss: AI value did not stop at code generation, because once coding sped up, adjacent coordination work became the new constraint. Its leadership lesson is also specific, not generic: adoption depended on managers using the tools themselves and on making AI fluency visible in hiring and promotion. For builders of enterprise copilots or agent workflows, this is a reminder that workflow integration and behavior change matter as much as model capability. The evidence is still a single-company case study from June 4, 2026, so the claims are actionable as of that date but should be treated as implementation guidance rather than proof of broad performance gains. The service-automation angle is real but secondary here: the article mentions legal, reporting, inbox, and coordination tasks, yet it does not provide rigorous outcomes for those functions.

## Limitations / open questions

This is a vendor-published case study, so it lacks independent benchmarks, control groups, cost data, or failure analysis. The article does not quantify delivery speed, quality, or ROI, so the scale of benefit is unclear. It also does not explain governance, security, data handling, or how Endava managed model risk across an 11,000-person workforce. DavaFlow is described at a high level, but the article does not specify the mechanics, templates, review gates, or tooling needed to replicate it. The claim that AI became the operating model itself is aspirational and not evaluated with external evidence.

## Contradictions / unverified claims

The article presents strong claims about redesigning delivery around agents, but most evidence is anecdotal and self-reported. Statements like being AI-native or having agents in the background may describe aspiration as much as measurable practice. The piece implies broad transformation across the organization, yet it does not show whether usage was deep, consistent, or sustainable across all teams. As with many vendor case studies, the narrative is polished and may overstate generalizability beyond Endava.
