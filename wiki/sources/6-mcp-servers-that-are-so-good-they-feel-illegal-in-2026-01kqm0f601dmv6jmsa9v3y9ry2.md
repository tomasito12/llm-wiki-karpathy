## Why it matters

The piece captures a common 2026 framing: protocol-based tool plugins let assistants do real work (web, repos, databases, social, execution) inside the same clients teams already use, which matters for internal automation and product design even when individual product claims are unverified.

## Implications for service-call automation

Operations can treat MCP as a pattern for wiring assistants to read-only internal APIs, ticketing or GitHub workflows, and controlled sandboxes—while explicitly constraining database write access and social-posting tools that do not match support policies.

## Context and Limitations

Promotional listicle format; no benchmarks, security review, or vendor-neutral comparison. Numeric ecosystem stats and governance claims in the closing section are asserted without citations in the article body.

## Contradictions / Unverified Claims

- Opening and bonus sections use hype (“top 1%”, “print money”, six-figure income) without evidence.
- Stats such as 97 million monthly MCP SDK downloads, 85,000 GitHub stars for Firecrawl, 500+ public MCP servers, and broad vendor/Linux Foundation governance claims are not sourced in the article.
- “Microsoft backs” GitHub MCP and endurance claims are editorial, not substantiated beyond naming Microsoft in the ecosystem.
