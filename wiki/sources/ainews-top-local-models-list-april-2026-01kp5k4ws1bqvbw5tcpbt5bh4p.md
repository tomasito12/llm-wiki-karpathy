# [AINews] Top Local Models List - April 2026

This article is a quick guide to which local language models people in online communities are recommending most. It is based on the author reading discussions in local model forums and noticing which model names keep coming up. Instead of trying to rank every model by test scores, it focuses on what people seem to find practical and worth using. The author says Qwen 3.5 is the broadest recommendation across different kinds of tasks. Gemma 4 is highlighted for being easy to use locally, especially in smaller and medium-sized setups. GLM-5 and GLM-4.7 are described as strong general open models, while MiniMax M2.5 and M2.7 are mentioned for tool-using and agent-like work. DeepSeek V3.2 is included as one of the strongest general open-weight models, and GPT-oss 20B is presented as a practical local choice, including uncensored variants. For coding, the author says the community strongly favors Qwen3-Coder-Next. The article also notes that many local model discussions are centered on roleplay and creative writing. As of 2026-04-14, this is best treated as a practical community snapshot rather than a hard technical ranking.
