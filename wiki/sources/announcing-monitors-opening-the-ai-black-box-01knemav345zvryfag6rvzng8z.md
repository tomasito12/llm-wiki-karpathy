Fin has launched Monitors, a new tool that helps businesses review and score AI and human conversations to ensure quality. Monitors let you choose which talks to check and set custom rules for evaluation, replacing random sampling. Together with Insights and Recommendations, Monitors give full control and clear visibility into AI-driven customer support.
