# Model lineage and post-training control are becoming enterprise selling points

## Signal

### Summary

The roundup frames Microsoft’s 'zero synthetic data' and 'zero distillation' stance as a way to keep model lineage and post-training fully under its control. It also ties this to 'Frontier Tuning' and company-specific agents that remain customer-controlled. The source presents this as a strategic enterprise message, not just a technical recipe.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-06-03, this matters because enterprise buyers often care about provenance, controllability, and dependence on external labs. The article suggests those concerns are being packaged directly into the model offering.

### Operational Relevance

Teams evaluating enterprise AI platforms should pay attention to whether the vendor exposes a controllable post-training path, because that affects customization, auditability, and ownership of adapted agents. The article also implies this can become a differentiator in procurement conversations.

### Service Automation Relevance

Relevant for service automation programs that need customer-specific behavior and tighter control over tuned assistants, but the source does not provide implementation evidence.

### Mentioned Entities

- Microsoft
- Frontier Tuning
- MAI-Thinking-1

### Suggested Destinations

- trends/

### Evidence Snippets

- "The clean lineage / no distillation emphasis is strategically significant. It addresses enterprise concerns around IP provenance, future controllability, and dependence on external labs"
- "It lets you customize our models to create custom, company-specific agents that only you control"
