# Coding-agent leaderboards are becoming system evaluations

## Signal

### Summary

Artificial Analysis replaced SWE-Bench Pro with DeepSWE to reduce benchmark gaming, and the rankings shifted materially. The roundup also notes that harness quality can change results even when the underlying model is unchanged. That makes leaderboard interpretation more fragile and more system-dependent.

### Why It Matters

Model comparisons for coding agents are increasingly confounded by benchmark choice, harness quality, routing, and hidden orchestration.

### Operational Relevance

Procurement and internal evals should isolate base model quality from wrapper behavior, and should not treat one leaderboard as a stable ground truth.

### Service Automation Relevance

For agentic support workflows, evaluation harnesses can hide important failures or inflate apparent performance; test the whole stack that will be deployed.
