# Vendors are racing to own the agent runtime boundary

## Signal

### Summary

Snowflake, Microsoft, Databricks, Salesforce, and ServiceNow are all presented as competing to become the layer between raw model capability and enterprise work. The article suggests this layer may move closer to the moment of execution, including devices, operating systems, and enterprise knowledge surfaces. The competitive boundary is therefore shifting from model access to runtime control.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-06-03, platform strategy may depend less on who has the best model and more on who controls the execution boundary and trust model. That affects how teams choose vendors and where they place orchestration, governance, and agent execution.

### Operational Relevance

Architectures may need to span warehouse, cloud, device, and OS surfaces. Teams should watch where vendors place the approval and execution boundary for agents.

### Service Automation Relevance

Customer support and back-office automation will likely be pulled into broader platform runtimes rather than isolated chatbot layers.
