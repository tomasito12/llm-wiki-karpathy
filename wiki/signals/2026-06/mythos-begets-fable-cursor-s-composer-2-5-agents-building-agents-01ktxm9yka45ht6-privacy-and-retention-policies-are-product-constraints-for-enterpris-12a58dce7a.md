# Privacy and retention policies are product constraints for enterprise desktop agents

## Signal

### Summary

The source says commercial desktop agents are avoided for highly confidential tasks because of data-retention policies and possible legal-privilege consequences. It also highlights OpenCoworker’s local-memory and local-model options as a privacy-oriented alternative. The operational lesson is that data handling is part of the product specification for agentic software.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-06-12, agent products that can read local documents or messages face trust barriers that are not present in ordinary chat apps.

### Operational Relevance

Teams shipping desktop or enterprise agents need explicit retention controls, local inference options, and clear data-flow documentation.

### Service Automation Relevance

Important for support systems that process confidential tickets, attachments, or internal messages, where retention and privilege exposure can block adoption.

### Mentioned Entities

- OpenCoworker
- Anthropic

### Evidence Snippets

- “I do not use existing commercial desktop agents for highly confidential tasks, since I’m uncomfortable with some of their data-retention policies…”
- “It saves its memory on your computer, and you can choose a LLM provider with a zero data-retention policy, local inference, or other options depending on your privacy requirements.”
