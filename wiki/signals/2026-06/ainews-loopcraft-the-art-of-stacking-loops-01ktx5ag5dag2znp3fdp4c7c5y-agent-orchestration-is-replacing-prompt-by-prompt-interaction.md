# Agent orchestration is replacing prompt-by-prompt interaction

## Signal

### Summary

The roundup argues that durable value comes from designing autonomous loops, not from repeatedly prompting a model. This shows up across agentic coding, research systems, managed runtimes, and review layers. The practical implication is that orchestration, handoff, and supervision become first-class design choices.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-06-12, teams that keep relying on interactive prompting may miss the leverage of repeatable agent loops. The source suggests that the engineering advantage is shifting toward workflow design and runtime control rather than better prompt writing alone.

### Operational Relevance

Build explicit loops for task decomposition, retries, evaluation, and handoff. Treat the model as one component inside a controlled execution system.

### Service Automation Relevance

Support and back-office automation can benefit from persistent loops, but only if escalation and review boundaries are designed into the workflow.
