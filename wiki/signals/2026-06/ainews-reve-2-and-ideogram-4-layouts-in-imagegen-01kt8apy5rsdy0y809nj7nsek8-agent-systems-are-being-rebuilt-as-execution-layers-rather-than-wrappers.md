# Agent systems are being rebuilt as execution layers rather than wrappers

## Signal

### Summary

The roundup repeatedly frames agents as harnesses, execution environments, and workflow loops instead of thin framework abstractions. Examples include the claim that the framework era is ending, MACU's multi-agent DAG framing, and tooling that adds observability, spend control, remote browsing, and orchestration. The operational pattern is a shift from prompting-centric prototypes to systems that manage tasks, tools, state, and monitoring explicitly.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-06-04, this is one of the clearest durable signals in the piece because it appears across multiple independent examples and product categories. The uncertainty is that the roundup aggregates commentary and launch claims rather than providing controlled comparisons, so the exact winning architecture remains unresolved.

### Operational Relevance

For AI engineering, the useful design unit is increasingly the harness: task decomposition, tool selection, monitoring, and context management. This matters for reliability, cost control, and post-deployment tuning of agentic systems.

### Service Automation Relevance

Highly relevant for support automation because it maps directly to workflow completion, escalation handling, browser/control layers, and operational observability in chat and voice assistants.
