# Sustained autonomous robotics uptime is becoming a demo target

## Signal

### Summary

Figure’s 24/7 sorting livestream is presented as evidence that robotics demos are shifting from isolated task completion to continuous autonomous uptime. The source says the system ran for 24+ hours without failure, with onboard execution and no teleoperation. The broader claim is less about one company and more about proving sustained operation under production-like conditions.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-05-15, long-duration autonomous operation is becoming a more meaningful robotics benchmark than short demo clips. For automation teams, the relevant question is whether a system can sustain uptime, reset from out-of-distribution cases, and maintain throughput without human intervention.

### Operational Relevance

This raises the bar for embodied automation evaluations: continuous uptime, recovery behavior, and onboard control matter more than single-episode success. It also suggests future robotics proof points will be operational rather than cinematic.

No direct service automation implications identified.
