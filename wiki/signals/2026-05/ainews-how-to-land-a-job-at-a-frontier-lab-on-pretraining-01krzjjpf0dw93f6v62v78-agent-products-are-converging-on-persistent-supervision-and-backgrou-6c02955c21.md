# Agent products are converging on persistent supervision and background execution

## Signal

### Summary

The roundup shows multiple agent products moving away from chat toward persistent, supervised workflows. Examples include trace-backed triage, remote execution, mobile supervision, and fan-out over longer tasks. The product shape is shifting toward systems that can run in the background and be monitored rather than one-shot conversational assistants.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-05-19, this suggests that agent product differentiation is increasingly determined by orchestration, persistence, and supervision mechanics. That matters because reliability and operational control become the core user experience, especially for coding and support automation.

### Operational Relevance

Teams building agents need trace capture, remote control, memory, and task decomposition as first-class features; model quality alone is not enough for production use.

### Service Automation Relevance

This points directly to service automation systems that can triage, escalate, and continue work across channels instead of dropping back to a chat transcript.
