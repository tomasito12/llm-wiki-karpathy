# Autonomous coding is moving from demo to internal default

## Signal

### Summary

Cognition says 89% of code committed inside the company is written by Devin, and the article uses that as evidence that autonomous software engineering has become routine inside at least one high-growth AI company. The signal is not just that the tool exists, but that it is shaping internal production output.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-05-31, this suggests agentic coding is being used as an operational default in a real company environment, not only as a showcase. That raises questions about review, quality control, and how much human engineering becomes supervisory.

### Operational Relevance

Teams evaluating coding agents should measure commit share, review burden, and defect escape rate, not just demo quality or benchmark scores.

### Service Automation Relevance

No direct service automation implications identified.
