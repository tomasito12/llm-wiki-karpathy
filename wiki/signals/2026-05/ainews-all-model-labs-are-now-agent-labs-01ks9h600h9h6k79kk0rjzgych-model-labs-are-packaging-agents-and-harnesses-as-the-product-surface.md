# Model labs are packaging agents and harnesses as the product surface

## Signal

### Summary

The roundup repeatedly frames model companies as moving up-stack from raw model releases to bundled agent products. It cites comments that "the model alone is no longer the product" and describes a product stack of model, harness, workflow, UI, memory, and economics. As of 2026-05-23, the operational takeaway is that evaluation and integration work around the model is part of product differentiation, not just a deployment detail.

### Why It Matters

This matters because it changes what teams should compare, buy, and build: model quality alone is insufficient evidence of product value. The source supports this with examples across OpenAI, Claude, AI21, and DeepSeek, but the roundup is still a mixed-evidence digest rather than a controlled study, so the thesis is plausible rather than proven.

### Operational Relevance

AI builders should expect more of the value to sit in harness design, workflow orchestration, memory, UI, and agent runtime choices. That means product evaluation should include tool discipline, remote execution behavior, and workflow completion, not just benchmark scores.

### Service Automation Relevance

Service automation stacks should be evaluated as end-to-end systems, not as model APIs. Chatbots and voicebots will likely need stronger orchestration, memory, and workflow completion layers to be competitive.
