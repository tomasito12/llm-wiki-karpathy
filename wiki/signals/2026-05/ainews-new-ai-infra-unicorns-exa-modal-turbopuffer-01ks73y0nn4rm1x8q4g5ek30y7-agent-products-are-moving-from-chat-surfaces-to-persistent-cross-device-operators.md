# Agent products are moving from chat surfaces to persistent cross-device operators

## Signal

### Summary

Codex is described as moving beyond an IDE-like chat workflow into remote computer use, richer context capture, and cross-device operation. The source emphasizes that Codex can securely use Mac apps from a phone even when the Mac is locked, alongside Appshots and org-level controls.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-05-22, this is a concrete sign that agent products are becoming operator surfaces, not just text interfaces. That raises the importance of permissions, context capture, and durable sessions for production use.

### Operational Relevance

Teams should design for persistent sessions, remote execution boundaries, and richer artifact capture if they want agents to operate across devices and apps.

### Service Automation Relevance

This matters for support automation because operators may delegate multi-step workflows to an agent that spans devices and app contexts, not just a single chat turn.
