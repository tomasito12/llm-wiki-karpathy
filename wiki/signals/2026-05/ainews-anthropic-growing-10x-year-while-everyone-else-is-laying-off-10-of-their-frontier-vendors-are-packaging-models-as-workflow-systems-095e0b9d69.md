# Frontier vendors are packaging models as workflow systems

## Signal

### Summary

The roundup describes OpenAI shipping a broad family of GPT-5.5, Codex, realtime, image, and cyber variants in a short period. The practical signal is that frontier vendors are selling model access together with workflow-specific surfaces, not just base model endpoints. For builders, this means integration design and product surface choice matter as much as model selection.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-05-09, product teams cannot treat the model as the full abstraction boundary. Vendor packaging, modality coverage, and task-specific flows are becoming part of the deployable system.

### Operational Relevance

Design around workflow surfaces, not just model APIs. Expect more value from orchestration, approvals, and modality routing than from swapping base models alone.

### Service Automation Relevance

Service automation teams should watch for vendor-provided workflow surfaces that compress integration work for support, coding, and realtime interaction.
