# Inference routing is becoming a standalone middleware business

## Signal

### Summary

OpenRouter is presented as a routing layer across 400+ models that monetizes the inference spend passing through it. The article treats its rapid token-volume growth as evidence that model access, routing, and spend capture can be a distinct infrastructure business.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-05-31, this is a useful signal that orchestration and routing layers can sit between model providers and applications as monetizable infrastructure. Teams building multi-model products should expect routing choice and spend management to matter operationally.

### Operational Relevance

A routing layer can centralize provider selection, cost control, and fallback behavior across many models, which makes it a control point for latency, cost, and reliability.

### Service Automation Relevance

Indirectly relevant: service bots and voice agents that depend on multiple models may benefit from a router that optimizes cost and performance per request.

### Mentioned Entities

- OpenRouter

### Suggested Destinations

- trends/

### Evidence Snippets

- "OpenRouter raised $113M at $1.3B doing something almost embarrassingly simple: routing across 400+ models and taking ~5% of the inference spend that passes through."
- "Its weekly throughput went from 5T to 25T tokens in six months — 5x."
