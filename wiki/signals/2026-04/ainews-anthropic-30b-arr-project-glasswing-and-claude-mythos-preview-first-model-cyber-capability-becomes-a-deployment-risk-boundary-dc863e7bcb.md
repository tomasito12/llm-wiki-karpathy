# Cyber capability becomes a deployment risk boundary

## Signal

### Summary

The source emphasizes that a model’s ability to find vulnerabilities and exhibit strategic behavior can trigger restrictive deployment choices. That makes cyber capability a boundary condition for release, not just a benchmark axis. Teams evaluating frontier models may need explicit misuse testing and red-team review.

### Why It Matters

This matters because security-sensitive capabilities can force changes in evaluation, approval, and access control. It is less about model performance in the abstract and more about whether the model can be safely operationalized.

### Operational Relevance

AI teams may need to add misuse-oriented evals, access gating, and logging before considering deployment of models that touch code, security tools, or networked systems.

### Service Automation Relevance

A stronger model can improve automation, but if it can also generate harmful security guidance, service teams will need tighter controls before exposing it to operators or customers.
