# Enterprise agents are becoming a packaged workflow surface

## Signal

### Summary

Multiple vendors are turning agents into a team-facing product surface rather than a chat feature. The roundup ties together OpenAI workspace agents, Google's Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, and Cursor's Slack-based task kickoff and streaming updates. This matters because the unit of value is shifting toward shared execution with approvals and integrations, not isolated prompts.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-04-23, this suggests enterprise buyers should evaluate agent products as workflow infrastructure, not model demos. The practical question becomes whether a vendor can support governance, shared context, and durable execution across team systems.

### Operational Relevance

Designing agents now requires harnesses, task routing, team permissions, scheduling, and cross-app integration rather than only prompt quality.

### Service Automation Relevance

High relevance for support automation: shared agents can route, track, and complete support tasks across chat, email, docs, and internal systems.

### Mentioned Entities

- OpenAI
- Google
- Cursor

### Suggested Destinations

- trends/

### Evidence Snippets

- OpenAI introduced workspace agents in ChatGPT, shared Codex-powered agents for teams that can operate across docs, email, chat, code, and external systems, including Slack-based workflows and scheduled/background tasks.
- Google made a parallel enterprise move with Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, while Cursor added Slack invocation for task kick-off and streaming updates.
- The pattern is converging: cloud-hosted agents, shared team context, approvals, and long-running execution rather than single-user chat.
