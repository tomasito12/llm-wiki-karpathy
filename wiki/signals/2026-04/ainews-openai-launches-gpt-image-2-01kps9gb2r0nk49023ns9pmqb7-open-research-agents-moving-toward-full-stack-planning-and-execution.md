# Open research agents moving toward full-stack planning and execution

## Signal

### Summary

The roundup describes research agents that do more than browse: they plan, search, run code, generate charts, and use internal data connectors. This is a fuller operational shape for research automation than simple query-answering or search summarization. It suggests the boundary between analyst tooling and autonomous agents is getting thinner.

### Why It Matters

For builders, the meaningful unit is becoming a research workflow that can orchestrate retrieval, code execution, and artifact generation in one run. That expands what internal research systems can automate, but also raises evaluation and governance needs.

### Operational Relevance

Teams building analyst assistants should expect the agent to need multimodal inputs, code execution, and access to internal corpora, not just web search. That creates stronger requirements for planning, logging, and error recovery.

### Service Automation Relevance

Indirect but real: the same planning-and-execution pattern can support back-office case handling and knowledge work, though the article does not show direct support-contact deployments.
