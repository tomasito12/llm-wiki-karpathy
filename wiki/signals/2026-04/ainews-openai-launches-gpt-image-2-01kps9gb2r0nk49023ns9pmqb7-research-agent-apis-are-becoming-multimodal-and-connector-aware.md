# Research-agent APIs are becoming multimodal and connector-aware

## Signal

### Summary

Google’s Deep Research update is described as a configurable API primitive with planning, arbitrary MCP support, multimodal inputs, code execution, chart generation, and progress streaming. That is a more complete research workflow surface than simple search or summarization APIs. The design points toward agents that can both reason and produce analyst-ready outputs.

### Why It Matters

This matters because it standardizes a higher-level research workflow that teams can plug into products and internal tools. It also raises the bar for evaluation, since the task is no longer just answer quality but end-to-end workflow completion.

### Operational Relevance

Implementers will need to handle connector permissions, artifact generation, and progress feedback in addition to basic retrieval. The API shape suggests research agents should be treated as orchestration systems, not just language-model wrappers.

No direct service automation implications identified.
