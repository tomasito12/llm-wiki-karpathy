# Simpler terminal-only agents may be enough for enterprise automation

## Signal

### Summary

One research summary reports that minimal coding agents with only a terminal and direct API access can perform as well as or better than more complex web- and tool-augmented agents for enterprise tasks. The article also says they are more cost-efficient and resilient.

### Why It Matters

If this holds as of 2026-04-05, enterprise automation design may favor simpler, more constrained agent harnesses over broad tool access. That reduces orchestration complexity and may improve reliability in production.

### Operational Relevance

Design automation around terminal access and direct APIs when possible instead of assuming a browser-heavy agent stack is required. Simpler harnesses may lower cost and improve resilience.

### Service Automation Relevance

For customer support and internal ops, terminal/API-first agents could handle back-office workflows more reliably than open-ended browser agents, especially when the task boundary is clear.

- ServiceNow
