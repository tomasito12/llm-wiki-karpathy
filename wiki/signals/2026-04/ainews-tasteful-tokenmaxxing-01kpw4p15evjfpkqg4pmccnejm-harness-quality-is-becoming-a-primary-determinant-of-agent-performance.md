# Harness quality is becoming a primary determinant of agent performance

## Signal

### Summary

The roundup repeatedly emphasizes that model results depend on the surrounding agent scaffold, not just the base model. It cites comments about harness mismatch, byo-key/model support in VS Code/Copilot, and enterprise demand for model flexibility across the SDLC. The operational lesson is that model selection and harness design need to be evaluated together.

### Why It Matters

As of 2026-04-23, the article supports treating harness design as a first-class engineering variable when comparing coding and agent systems. This is durable because it affects benchmarking, vendor choice, and production reliability.

### Operational Relevance

Teams should benchmark models inside the target scaffold, not in isolation, and should expect provider-specific harnesses to shape real task success.

### Service Automation Relevance

Support and back-office agents will vary materially based on workflow scaffolds, approval steps, and integrations, so harness design affects automation quality directly.

### Evidence Snippets

- Developer ergonomics around harness/model independence improved: VS Code/Copilot rolled out bring-your-own-key/model support across plans and business/enterprise, enabling providers like Anthropic, Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Azure, Ollama, and local backends.
- most models still seem overfit to their own agent harnesses.
- enterprise buyers want model flexibility and infrastructure that spans the full SDLC, not attachment to one lab.
