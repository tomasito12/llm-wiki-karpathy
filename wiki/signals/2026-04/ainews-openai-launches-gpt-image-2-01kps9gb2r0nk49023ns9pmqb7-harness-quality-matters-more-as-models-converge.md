# Harness quality matters more as models converge

## Signal

### Summary

The roundup repeatedly emphasizes runtimes, harnesses, and orchestration as the decisive layer in agent systems. As open-weight and proprietary models get closer on certain tasks, the surrounding system becomes a major source of differentiation. That makes tracing, permissions, memory, and subagent control more important engineering investments.

### Why It Matters

This shifts attention away from single-model selection and toward the architecture that surrounds model calls. Teams that ignore harness quality may miss reliability gains even when model capability is strong.

### Operational Relevance

Practical agent systems need memory, tool routing, permissions, evaluation hooks, and failure recovery. The roundup suggests those layers are increasingly where engineering effort pays off.

### Service Automation Relevance

This is highly relevant for support automation because containment, escalation, and safe task completion depend on runtime controls as much as on model quality.
