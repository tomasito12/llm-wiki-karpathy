# GroundVue's Public-Meeting Knowledge Collection Workflow

## Implementation Study

### Overview

GroundVue uses Codex to search across public meeting videos, websites, and local platforms, then continuously collect and organize information at scale. The company frames this as a way to help governments learn from one another by making dispersed public records searchable and comparable.

### What was implemented?

A Codex-assisted workflow for finding hard-to-reach public sources and building systems that continuously collect and organize them.

### Business objective

To turn scattered public information into usable institutional knowledge and do work that would otherwise require much larger groups of technologists and researchers.

### Technical approach

The source says GroundVue uses Codex to find hard-to-reach public sources and build systems that continuously collect and organize them.

### Deployment context

The workflow operates across roughly 90,000 government bodies, with information scattered across videos, websites, and local platforms.

### Outcome / current status

Ongoing as described; the source says tasks that once took days or weeks now take minutes.

### Why it succeeded or struggled

It appears to work because Codex helps bridge fragmented public sources and automate collection and organization work that is otherwise time-consuming.

### Operational constraints

The main constraint is source fragmentation across many public platforms and formats, which makes manual collection slow and expensive.

### AI / model observations

This case suggests AI is most useful when the hard part is assembling and normalizing information spread across many weakly structured sources.

### Implications for service automation

Indirect but relevant: similar methods could support public-sector service operations, record search, and internal knowledge retrieval, but the source does not describe a direct customer-support deployment.

### Strategic signals

Small teams can use agentic tools to replace labor-intensive information compilation work, especially in public-sector-adjacent knowledge systems.

### Key Lessons

- Fragmented public data becomes more tractable when the tool can search and organize across many source types.
- The biggest win is not just drafting, but building a continuous collection pipeline.
- One small team can cover a much larger information surface when the workflow is automated.

### Open Questions

- How reliable is the collected data across changing public platforms?
- What human review is needed before the information is used operationally?
